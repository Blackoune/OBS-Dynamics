OBS Dynamics switches your OBS scenes based on the game on screen.

Before you install
- OBS Studio 28 or later, with the WebSocket server enabled (Tools > WebSocket Server Settings).
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Your data
- Everything stays on your computer: no account, no ads, no data sent to Blackoune.
- Your settings are stored in %APPDATA%. Uninstalling does not delete them.
